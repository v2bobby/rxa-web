The agreement

Last updated 15 August 2026

1. Not medical advice

RxLoop provides general safety and adherence information. It is not a diagnosis, not a prescription, and not a substitute for a pharmacist, physician or emergency service. It deliberately contains no dosing figures. If anything here conflicts with what a qualified clinician has told you, follow the clinician. In an emergency, seek care immediately rather than consulting this app.

2. Content is a draft under review

The reference entries and their translations into Nigerian Pidgin, Yorùbá, Hausa and Igbo are first-pass drafts that have not yet completed licensed clinical and native-speaker review. The app labels this on every entry. Treat it accordingly.

3. Reports are unverified

A suspect-pack report records one person's observation. It is not a finding that a product is falsified, and it is not a regulatory determination. Only a competent authority, such as NAFDAC, can make that determination. Do not use reports to make public accusations about a named seller or manufacturer.

4. Reminders are best-effort

Reminders are local to your device and depend on your browser and operating system. They may not fire if the app is closed, storage is cleared, or notification permission is refused. Do not rely on them as the only safeguard for a critical medicine.

5. Acceptable use

Do not submit knowingly false reports, attempt to overload or interfere with the service, or reuse the content to imply verification or endorsement that does not exist.

6. Availability and changes

The service is provided as-is and may change or be unavailable without notice. Features described as roadmap are not commitments or delivery dates.

7. Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, RxLoop is not liable for loss arising from reliance on the information provided. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

8. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
