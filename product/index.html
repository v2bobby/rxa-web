Available in the app today

Medication Reference

General safety and adherence information — what a medication is for, precautions, and when to seek help — for common medications including Paracetamol, Amoxicillin, Coartem, Ibuprofen, Metronidazole, and ORS. Deliberately excludes numeric dosing, which varies by patient and should always come from a prescription label or pharmacist.

Five Languages

Every entry is available in English, Yoruba, Hausa, Igbo, and Nigerian Pidgin — switchable instantly in the app.

Works Offline

RxLoop is a Progressive Web App with a real service worker — install it once, and medication lookup keeps working without a connection.

Counterfeit Reporting

Log a suspected counterfeit — photo, batch code, purchase location, and notes — directly from your phone. Reports are currently saved on-device; cross-device sync is on the roadmap below.

Adherence Reminders

Set a daily reminder for a medication course. Works via local notifications while RxLoop is installed on your device.

Roadmap
Being built toward the Nigeria pilot

Computer-Vision Counterfeit Detection

Instant packaging verification — holograms, serial numbers, batch codes — checked against a manufacturer-verified database. This requires real partnerships with manufacturers and regulators, which we're building toward rather than simulating.

Synced Community Reports

Counterfeit reports shared and aggregated across users and locations, turning individual reports into a real early-warning map.

Telemedicine Consultations

Escalate to a licensed pharmacist for a live consultation at a transparent flat fee.

Pharmacy & Health-System Dashboards

B2B tools giving pharmacies and clinics visibility into adherence and population-level insight.
