Last updated 15 August 2026

What the app stores

Your suspect-pack reports, your reminder courses and your language choice are saved in your browser's local storage, on your device. They are not transmitted to RxLoop or anyone else, because there is currently no backend to receive them.

What we can see

Nothing about your medicines, reports or reminders. Our host records standard server logs for pages served, which include IP address and browser type, as any web server does. We do not attach these to any individual and do not use them for advertising.

Photographs

If you attach a photo to a report, only its filename is stored in the report record on your device. The image itself is never uploaded.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means Google's servers see the request for the font file. Nothing about your health data is included. There are no advertising or tracking scripts on this site.

Deleting your data

Use Clear all in the app's report ledger, or clear site data in your browser settings. Because storage is device-local, that removes it completely — we hold no copy to delete.

When this changes

Cross-device sync is on the roadmap. When reports begin leaving devices, this page will be rewritten before that ships, and the app will ask for explicit consent rather than assume it.
