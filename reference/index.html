Paracetamol

Acetaminophen · pain and fever

What it is for
Brings down fever and eases mild to moderate pain.
How to take it
Swallow with water. Never take two different products that both contain paracetamol — check cold and flu mixtures, which very often do.
When to get help
Fever still there after three days, or any pain lasting more than five days. Get help immediately if too much has been taken, even if the person feels fine.

When to find help
If fever still dey after three days, or pain pass five days. If person take too much, rush go hospital sharp-sharp even if e feel alright.

Amoxicillin

Antibiotic · bacterial infection

What it is for
An antibiotic for certain bacterial infections. It does nothing at all for colds, flu or most sore throats.
How to take it
Space the doses evenly and finish the entire course even after you feel well. Stopping early is what breeds resistant bacteria.
When to get help
Rash, swelling of the face or lips, or trouble breathing — stop and get help immediately. Also return if there is no improvement after three days.
Nigerian Pidgin
Wetin e dey do

How to take am
Space the dose well-well and finish the whole course even after you don feel better. Na to stop am early dey make bacteria strong pass medicine.
When to find help
If rash show, face or lip swell, or breathing hard you — stop and find help sharp-sharp. Come back too if nothing change after three days.

Artemether–Lumefantrine

ACT · uncomplicated malaria

What it is for
First-line treatment for uncomplicated malaria. It is only correct after a test confirms malaria — treating a fever blind wastes the drug and hides the real cause.
How to take it
Take it with food or something fatty like milk or groundnut, otherwise the body absorbs far too little. Complete every dose in the pack.
When to get help
Vomiting within an hour of a dose, convulsions, confusion, very dark urine, or a fever that returns after finishing — these need a clinic the same day.
Nigerian Pidgin
Wetin e dey do
Na the first medicine for ordinary malaria. E only correct after test confirm malaria — to just treat fever anyhow dey waste medicine and dey hide the real problem.
How to take am
Take am with food or something wey get oil like milk or groundnut, if not body no go absorb am well. Finish every dose inside the pack.
When to find help
If you vomit within one hour after dose, or convulsion, confusion, very dark urine, or fever come back after you finish — go clinic that same day.

Mu ún pẹ̀lú oúnjẹ tàbí ohun tí ó ní òróró bíi wàrà tàbí èpà, bí bẹ́ẹ̀ kọ́ ara kò ní gbà á dáadáa. Parí gbogbo ìwọ̀n tí ó wà nínú àpò náà.

ORS + Zinc

Oral rehydration · childhood diarrhoea

What it is for
Replaces the water and salts lost in diarrhoea. This is the treatment that saves the child — the fluid, not an antibiotic.
How to take it
Mix one sachet into the exact volume of clean water printed on it. Never mix it stronger, never sweeten it, and give small sips continuously. Zinc continues for the full ten to fourteen days.
When to get help
Sunken eyes, no tears, no urine for many hours, a child too weak to drink, blood in the stool, or diarrhoea past two days — this is an emergency.
Nigerian Pidgin
Wetin e dey do
E dey replace the water and salt wey body loss for running belle. Na the fluid dey save the pikin, no be antibiotic.
How to take am
Mix one sachet inside the exact water wey dem write for the pack. No make am strong pass, no add sugar, give small-small sip continuously. Zinc go continue full ten to fourteen days.
When to find help
If eye don sink, no tears, no urine for many hours, pikin too weak to drink, blood dey inside toilet, or e pass two days — na emergency, run go hospital.

Bí a ṣe ń lò ó

Amlodipine

Antihypertensive · daily, long-term

What it is for
Lowers blood pressure. It works only while it is being taken — high blood pressure has no symptoms, so feeling fine is not a reason to stop.
How to take it
Take it at the same time every day, with or without food. If a day is missed, take the next dose at its normal time rather than doubling up.
When to get help
Swelling of the ankles, dizziness on standing, or a very slow pulse are worth reporting. Chest pain or breathlessness needs urgent care.

How to take am
Take am same time every day, with or without food. If you miss one day, take the next dose for im normal time; no double am.
When to find help
If ankle swell, head dey turn when you stand up, or heartbeat slow well-well, tell person. Chest pain or breathing wahala na urgent.

Metformin

Type 2 diabetes · daily, long-term

What it is for
Helps the body use its own insulin better in type 2 diabetes. It works alongside food and movement, not instead of them.
How to take it
Take it with or just after a meal — this reduces the stomach upset most people get in the first weeks. Do not skip meals while taking it.
When to get help
Persistent vomiting, deep fast breathing, unusual muscle pain or extreme weakness need same-day care. Shaking, sweating and confusion mean low sugar — take something sweet and get help.
Nigerian Pidgin
Wetin e dey do

How to take am
Take am with food or just after you chop — e go reduce the belle wahala wey plenty people dey get for the first weeks. No skip food while you dey take am.
When to find help
If vomit no gree stop, breathing deep and fast, strange muscle pain or serious weakness — go hospital that day. If you dey shake, sweat and confuse, na low sugar: take something sweet quick and find help.

Ibuprofen

NSAID · pain, swelling, fever

What it is for
Eases pain, brings down fever and reduces swelling — useful where paracetamol alone is not enough.
How to take it
Always take it with food or just after eating, never on an empty stomach. Do not combine it with diclofenac, aspirin or any other anti-inflammatory, and avoid it entirely if you have an ulcer, kidney disease, or are late in pregnancy.
When to get help
Black tarry stools, vomiting blood, severe stomach pain, swelling of the face, or passing much less urine than usual — stop and get help the same day.
Nigerian Pidgin

When to find help
Black toilet like tar, vomit wey get blood, serious belle pain, face swell, or you no dey urinate like before — stop am and find help that same day.

Tramadol

Opioid painkiller · prescription only

What it is for
A strong prescription painkiller for short-term severe pain. It is not a stamina aid, an energy tablet, or something to take for everyday aches — those uses are where the harm in this country is concentrated.
How to take it
Take it only as a clinician prescribed, for as short a time as possible. Never combine it with alcohol, sedatives or codeine syrups. Stopping suddenly after weeks of use causes withdrawal, so a clinician should taper it.
When to get help
Slow or shallow breathing, extreme drowsiness, blue lips, fits, or confusion are an overdose emergency — get help immediately. If you find you cannot get through a day without it, that is dependence and it is treatable; ask for help without shame.
Nigerian Pidgin
Wetin e dey do
Na strong prescription painkiller for serious pain wey no go tey. E no be stamina medicine, no be energy tablet, and no be for ordinary body pain — na those uses cause most of the wahala for this country.
How to take am
Take am only as doctor prescribe, and for the shortest time possible. Never mix am with alcohol, sedative or codeine syrup. If you don dey take am for weeks, no just stop sharp — make doctor reduce am small-small.
When to find help
Slow or shallow breathing, serious sleepiness, blue lips, convulsion, or confusion na overdose emergency — find help immediately. If you no fit pass one day without am, na dependence, and dem fit treat am; ask for help, no shame dey there.

Ohun tí ó ń ṣe
Oògùn ìrora líle tí oníṣègùn gbọ́dọ̀ kọ. Kì í ṣe oògùn agbára, kì í sì í ṣe fún ìrora ojoojúmọ́ — ìlò bẹ́ẹ̀ ni ó ń fa ìpalára jùlọ ní orílẹ̀-èdè yìí.
Bí a ṣe ń lò ó
Lò ó gẹ́gẹ́ bí oníṣègùn ṣe kọ nìkan, fún àkókò kúkúrú. Má ṣe da á pọ̀ mọ́ ọtí, oògùn oorun tàbí sìírọ́pù codeine. Bí o bá ti lò ó fún ọ̀sẹ̀ púpọ̀, má ṣe dá dúró lójijì — jẹ́ kí oníṣègùn dín in kù díẹ̀díẹ̀.
Ìgbà tí o gbọ́dọ̀ wá ìrànlọ́wọ́
Mímí tí ó lọ́ra, oorun àṣejù, ètè búlúù, gìrì, tàbí ìdàrúdàpọ̀ ọkàn jẹ́ pàjáwìrì — wá ìrànlọ́wọ́ lẹ́sẹ̀kẹsẹ̀. Bí o kò bá lè lo ọjọ́ kan láìsí i, ìgbẹ́kẹ̀lé ni, a sì lè tọ́jú rẹ̀; béèrè fún ìrànlọ́wọ́ láìtijú.
Hausa
Abin da yake yi
Maganin ciwo mai ƙarfi da likita kaɗai ke rubutawa, na ciwo mai tsanani na ɗan lokaci. Ba maganin ƙarfi ba ne, ba kuma na ciwon yau da kullum ba — irin wannan amfani shi ke haifar da mafi yawan illa a wannan ƙasa.
Yadda ake sha
A sha yadda likita ya rubuta kawai, na ɗan gajeren lokaci. Kada a haɗa shi da barasa, maganin barci ko sirop na codeine. Idan an sha shi makonni, kada a daina kwatsam — likita ya rage a hankali.
Lokacin neman taimako
Numfashi a hankali ko mara zurfi, matsanancin barci, leɓe shuɗi, farfaɗiya, ko rikicewa gaggawa ce ta yawan sha — a nemi taimako nan take. Idan ba za ka iya wuce rana ba tare da shi ba, jaraba ce, ana kuma iya magance ta; a nemi taimako ba tare da kunya ba.
