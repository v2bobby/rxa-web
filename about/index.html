About RxLoop

Closing the gap between prescription and safe treatment.

Why RxLoop Exists

In Sub-Saharan Africa, the pharmacist-to-patient ratio in rural areas can exceed 1:10,000, and an estimated 16.7% of the regional drug supply is counterfeit or substandard. Most people never get professional guidance on the medication they take — they get it from an informal vendor, in a language that may not be their own, with no way to verify what's actually in the package.

RxLoop exists to close that gap: a digital pharmacist in every pocket, built for the connectivity and language realities of the communities it serves, not adapted from a Western template.

What We're Building

RxLoop is medication-safety infrastructure, built in stages. Today, that means a real, working tool: medication reference information in five languages, offline-first so it works without reliable connectivity, and a community counterfeit-reporting feature that starts building toward verified detection.

The longer-term vision — computer-vision counterfeit detection against a manufacturer-verified database — depends on real partnerships with manufacturers and regulators, which we're building toward deliberately rather than claiming before they exist.
